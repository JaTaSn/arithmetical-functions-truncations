DELIMITERS: $...$, NOT \(...\). This is the one place in the workspace where the
house convention is wrong. arXiv's abstract pages load MathJax with an explicit
override -- verified 2026-08-30 by reading their own configuration at
arxiv.org/static/browse/0.3.4/js/mathjaxToggle.min.js:

    tex2jax: { inlineMath: [['$','$']], processEscapes: true, ... }

Replacing the inline delimiter list REMOVES MathJax's default \(...\), so a
\(x\) in an abstract renders as the literal characters backslash-paren-x. Only
displayMath was left at its default, so \[...\] probably still works -- but
"probably" is not a good bet on a field you cannot preview, hence $...$ throughout,
including the displayed line, which is indented so arXiv keeps it on its own line.

NO BARE ~ FOR "IS ASYMPTOTIC TO". Inside math, ~ is a non-breaking space, so the
relation would simply vanish and the formula would read as two expressions with a
gap. Write \sim. (arXiv separately asks that ~ be omitted from abstracts as a
TeX-ism, which is about its use as a tie -- a different problem with the same
character.)

Every macro below is in AMSmath/AMSsymbols, which arXiv's MathJax loads; checked
by compiling them.

Let $K$ be a field containing $\mathbb{Q}$, let $\Gamma$ be the ring of all
functions from the positive integers to $K$ under Dirichlet convolution, and let
$\Gamma_n$ be its truncation to functions supported on $[1,n]$. In [Snellman,
Homology Homotopy Appl. 2 (2000), 17-27; arXiv:math/9904143] it was shown that
$\Gamma_n$ is a polynomial ring modulo a monomial ideal $I_n$ which is stable
after reversing the order of the variables, and the Poincare-Betti series of
$\Gamma_n$ was computed in terms of the numbers $C_{n,v}$ of minimal generators
of $I_n$ of least support $v$.

We prove that $C_{n,v} = \Phi(n,p_v)$, Legendre's sifting function: the number of
integers in $[1,n]$ free of prime factors $\le p_v$. This identifies an invariant
of a minimal free resolution with a classical object of sieve theory. As
consequences we obtain: a proof of Conjecture 4.6 of the 2000 paper, which was
left open there; the average order $C_n \sim \pi(n)^2/2$ of the total number of
minimal generators, showing that the lower bound $C_n \ge \binom{\pi(n)+1}{2}$ of
that paper is asymptotically sharp, together with the exact order

    $C_n - \binom{\pi(n)+1}{2} \sim \frac{16}{3} n^{3/2} / \log^3 n$

of the error; and the identification of $C_n$ with the OEIS sequence A182843. We
also record errata for the 2000 paper: one stated result is false, and two proofs
are incomplete. Corrected statements and complete proofs are given.

  11N25  Distribution of integers with specified multiplicative constraints
  13D40  Hilbert-Samuel and Hilbert-Kunz functions; Poincare series
  13F55  Face and Stanley-Reisner rings; simplicial complexes

NOTE ON THE SCHEME: this is MSC **2020**, the current revision. MSC 2000 was
superseded in 2010 and again in 2020; the AMS and zbMATH both index under 2020,
and amsart's \subjclass takes the year as an argument, so a 2000-scheme code
would be both flagged and wrong. The same codes are now in the manuscript itself
as \subjclass[2020]{...}.

Primary 13D02 rather than 11N25 is a deliberate choice: the object studied is a
minimal free resolution, and the sieve function is what its invariants turn out
to equal. The paper is about the resolution, stated in the language of the sieve,
not a contribution to sieve theory. If you disagree, swapping 13D02 and 11N25 is
defensible and costs one edit in two places.

    truncations-revisited.tex     the source; arXiv compiles it

    S2-ratio.pdf                  figure 2

Do NOT upload: truncations-revisited.pdf, .aux, .log, .out, .synctex.gz. arXiv
builds its own PDF, and a stale one in the bundle is how you submit the wrong
version. These are gitignored, so a `git archive` of the directory is already
clean -- but a plain zip of the working directory is not, because the build
products are sitting there locally. Also exclude README.md, abstract.txt and this
file: they are repository bookkeeping, not part of the paper.

Engine: the manuscript declares xelatex (two passes). arXiv's default is pdflatex,
so if the build fails there, add a file named `00README.XXX` containing

    engine xelatex

or check whether the document compiles under pdflatex unchanged -- it uses only
amsart, amsthm/amsmath/amssymb, hyperref, orcidlink, graphicx and booktabs, none
of which require XeTeX, but the UTF-8 author names (Jurgen, Gerald, Poincare)
will need inputenc under pdflatex.
