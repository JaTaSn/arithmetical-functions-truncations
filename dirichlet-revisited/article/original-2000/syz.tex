\begin{section}{Syzygies in \(\Gamma_n\)}
  The set of all such syzygies form a free \(\Gamma_n\) module.
  From our previous results, we see that the rank of this free module
  is given by 
  \begin{align*}
    \beta_1 & = \sum_{i=1}^r C_{n,1+r-i} (i-1) \\
    & \ge \sum_{i=1}^r i (i-1) \\
    & = \frac{1}{3} (r-1)r(r+1)
  \end{align*}
  
  Similarly, for the \(q\)'th Betti number we get that 
  \begin{align*}
    \beta_q & = \sum_{i=1}^r C_{n,1+r-i} \binom{i-1}{q} \\
    & \ge \sum_{i=1}^r i \binom{i-1}{q} \\
    & = {\frac {\left (r-q\right )\left (r+1\right ){r\choose q}}{2+q}}
  \end{align*}

\end{section}
